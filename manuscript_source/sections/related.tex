\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Private tolling schemes include VPriv, PrETP, PriPAYD, and other route and audit designs~\cite{vpriv2009,pretp2010,pripayd2011}. Private electronic road pricing based on Bulletproofs and vector commitments checks bounds and commitments under a distinct statement interface~\cite{desmet2024}. TollVeil addresses collateral under partial authenticated observations and a coupled graph-dependent envelope.

Fair exchange, payment channels and incremental computation offer different mechanisms for authorization or repeated proofs~\cite{fairstream2023,cape2025,fairrelay2026,micronova2025}. Their measured operations are listed separately from authenticated toll settlement. The finite-prior analysis follows quantitative information flow~\cite{smith2009qif,alvim2012g,alvim2020book}. The equality graph addresses the added effect of a permitted terminal intervention on a stream of cumulative amounts.
