\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

TollVeil certifies coupled fee envelopes and admits refunds against a stored predecessor in the same transaction that credits the pool. An equality graph gives the greatest safe release on a specified finite stopping support. The aligned traces and release scores quantify the cost of single and repeated amount disclosure. Full-corridor clients retain opened witnesses for their active sessions, while anchored local updates can use commitments for untouched siblings. The evaluation leaves live transport, device limits and external payments as deployment questions.
