\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

Solvency is conditional on the legal graph, honest enrollment and authentic measurement history. Sensor spoofing, loss of terminal evidence and disagreement about a bulletin require operational dispute and recovery procedures. The prototype uses local replay clocks and test keys. A deployment also needs a trusted clock, broadcaster availability, custody of keys and a policy for retained evidence.

The release results describe a finite support and retrospective trace timing. Even account aliases and packet lengths can constrain anonymity when linkable. The transport bound is conditional on a joint scheduling interface, and the two modeled delay profiles characterize one component. Buffering can trade some liquidity for less timing leakage. Measuring such a system under cross-session scheduling and handling unavailable messages remains separate from the report's proof timings.
