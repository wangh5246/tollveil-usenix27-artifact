# Proof-gated escrow replay

The registrar authorizes an enrollment over the session, corridor policy, entry, vehicle class, device key commitment, deposit, genesis commitment and epoch. Provisioning fixes the initial envelope for each supported entry and class from the registered graph and tariff. The ledger verifies the registrar signature and checks the deposit against its policy table before reserving funds.

The ledger uses the current session row to reconstruct the entire Groth16 public vector. The relation is selected by the registered corridor family and operation. Each relation key is pinned to the byte hash of its verification key and provenance for its compiled relation. The verifier process loads that registry before handling requests.

An admission holds a SQLite writer reservation from reading the session row through verifying the proof, advancing the row by revision and state comparison, adjusting the fleet account, and persisting the receipt. The terminal path verifies the stored, scope-bound Ed25519 bulletin. The ledger fixes one bulletin per policy and epoch. The trusted replay scheduler supplies the admission clock. For the eight-slot corridors the ledger encodes the early eligibility flag above the eight bulletin-mask bits. An early close proof has a fully elapsed authenticated checkpoint and all eight openings. A timed close can settle a residual envelope under the authorized fault policy.

An admitted revision retains the canonical statement and its receipt. A retry at the same revision verifies its proof against that statement and receives the original receipt. Two valid proofs for the same statement therefore produce one credit under concurrent submission. A rejection rolls back the transaction.

The two eight-slot corridor relations commit the previous authenticated prefix digest inside the predecessor state. The circuit recomputes that prefix from the current signed checkpoint at the stored predecessor count. The proof uses earlier openings and their salts throughout a session. The local gap continuation reads one gap opening and the digest and aggregate cap of each untouched sibling subtree. A later raw gap close uses the corresponding preimages.

For the sixteen-slot gap fixture, an authoritative raw or eligible stateful close proof certifies its proposed terminal amount and digest. The ledger stores the digest and its statement at the same revision and scope. Digest-only close checks the stored certificate before executing the pairing check and credit. The stateful certificate relation requires at least three opened positions in every four-slot gap. The raw certificate path covers weaker opening patterns and its total includes certificate generation, admission, digest proof generation and close. The registrar enrolls correctly bounded leaf caps. Each local update verifies the sibling paths against the prior root, preserves untouched leaf commitments, and computes a new bounded cap for the changed gap.

The corridor replays use archived real-derived cohorts, fresh session signing keys and salts, and fixture-scripted checkpoints and infrastructure bulletins. The gap fixture uses a synthetic anchored corridor. Accounts and timing refer to the local SQLite replay. The real-derived inputs, proving witnesses and operational databases stay on the replay host.
